NEM dispatch simulator

Pick an interval: what the market did, what each rung said, and why they differ. Times are interval ending, NEM time (AEST, UTC+10). Prices $/MWh, volumes MW. Every modelled price is the scored result in results/; only rungs 3b and 4 are re-solved, for the interval on screen, to show their flows.
